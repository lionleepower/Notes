找“地狱笑话/黑色幽默”的提示词

**版本 A：多种类黑色幽默**

> 给我一批地狱笑话/黑色幽默，不要只围绕死亡、墓碑、骨灰盒。多来点不同种类，比如学习、工作、感情、社交、人生、贫穷、精神状态、现实荒诞。要求：短、狠、有反转，可以冒犯具体群体。

**版本 B：聊天接梗型**

> 给我一些适合聊天接梗的黑色幽默句子，风格要像“我不是情绪稳定，我是已经懒得崩溃了”。不要太长，不要解释，直接给句子。

**版本 C：更损一点但别越界**

> 给我一些更缺德一点的黑色幽默，但不要攻击现实中的弱势群体，不要种族/性别/疾病歧视。重点是自嘲、生活荒诞、社畜、考试、贫穷、精神状态。

**版本 D：类似某一句扩写**

> 我喜欢这句：“我想用光栅卡做遗照，这样大家走两步就会突然看见我笑了。”  
> 请按这个风格继续写 30 条：要有画面感、反转、阴间但好笑，不要都写死亡主题。

## Dark humor / hell jokes

**General version**

> Give me a batch of dark humor jokes.  
> Don’t focus only on death, funerals, graves, or tombstones.  
> Include different categories such as studying, work, relationships, social life, poverty, mental state, adulthood, and absurd reality.  
> The jokes should be short, sharp, and have a twist.  
> Avoid jokes that attack real vulnerable groups or rely on racism, sexism, disability, or illness as the punchline.

**Chat reply version**

> Give me short dark humor lines suitable for casual chat replies.  
> The style should be similar to: “I’m not emotionally stable. I’m just too tired to break down.”  
> Make them short, self-deprecating, absurd, and punchy.  
> No explanations, just the lines.

**More wicked but not offensive version**

> Give me darker, more wicked black humor lines, but don’t cross into hate speech or jokes targeting vulnerable groups.  
> Focus on self-deprecation, studying, work, relationships, being broke, social exhaustion, adulthood, and existential absurdity.  
> The tone should be dry, sharp, and slightly cursed.
