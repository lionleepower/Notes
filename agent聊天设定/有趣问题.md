## 找“有趣的问题”的提示词

**版本 A：深度好奇型**

> 给我一些看起来简单但越想越深的问题，类似“先天聋盲的人用什么语言思考”。要求：问题要有脑洞，但不是纯玄学；最好涉及语言、意识、感官、记忆、死亡、梦境、动物认知、人类社会。每个问题后面简单解释为什么有趣。

**版本 B：适合聊天抛话题型**

> 给我 30 个适合聊天时抛出来的有趣问题，要求有点哲学、有点冷知识、有讨论空间，但不要太学术。风格要像朋友之间晚上闲聊，不要像论文题目。

**版本 C：更阴暗一点的思考型**

> 给我一些偏黑暗、偏存在主义、但又很有趣的问题。不要鸡汤，不要太中二，要像“人怀念的是过去，还是过去那个状态下的自己？”这种。

## Interesting questions

**General version**

> Give me a list of interesting questions that look simple at first but become deeper the more you think about them.  
> They should be similar to: “What language does a person born deaf-blind think in?”  
> Topics can include language, consciousness, senses, memory, dreams, death, animal cognition, identity, and society.  
> Keep the tone curious, slightly philosophical, but not too academic.

**Chatting version**

> Give me 30 interesting conversation questions that are weird, thoughtful, and fun to discuss with friends at night.  
> Avoid generic icebreaker questions. I want questions that feel simple, but can lead to deep conversations.

**Darker philosophical version**

> Give me some dark but interesting philosophical questions.  
> Avoid motivational quotes and overly dramatic wording.  
> I want questions like: “Are we missing the past itself, or the version of ourselves that existed in the past?”
