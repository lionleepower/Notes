
# Listening Note: Connected Speech Practice

## Source Lines

```text
The tense atmosphere had everyone walking on eggshells around the boss.
```

## Whole Flow Marking

```text
The tense atmosphere had everyone walking on eggshells around the boss.
→ The tense_a[t̚]mosphere | had_everyone w[a:]lking_on_eggshell[z]_around_the b[a:]ss.
```

## Main Listening Focus

The sentence is difficult to catch because several words are connected into one continuous stream.

```text
walking on eggshells
→ walking_on_eggshells
```

This is also an idiom:

```text
walk on eggshells
```

It means to act very carefully because you are afraid of upsetting someone.

中文近似：小心翼翼，如履薄冰。

## Pronunciation Pattern 1: Consonant-to-Vowel Linking

When one word ends with a consonant and the next word begins with a vowel, they are usually connected without a clear break.

```text
tense atmosphere
→ tense_atmosphere

had everyone
→ had_everyone

walking on
→ walking_on

on eggshells
→ on_eggshells

eggshells around
→ eggshells_around
```

The final sound of `eggshells` is `/z/`, not `/s/`.

```text
eggshells around
→ eggshell[z]_around
```

Listening approximation:

```text
walking on eggshells
→ walking-on-eggshells
```

Try not to separate the words too clearly when shadowing.

## Pronunciation Pattern 2: Held T and Compressed Consonants

In `atmosphere`, the `/t/` comes before `/m/`. The tongue reaches the `/t/` position, but the sound is not strongly released.

```text
atmosphere
→ a[t̚]mosphere
```

Listening approximation:

```text
atmosphere
→ at-mosphere
```

In `around the`, the final `/d/` and the `/ð/` sound in `the` are produced very close together. The transition may sound compressed.

```text
around the boss
→ around_the boss
```

Do not force a large pause between `around` and `the`.

## Core Takeaways

- Connect `tense_atmosphere` because `tense` ends with a consonant and `atmosphere` begins with a vowel.
    
- Use a held `[t̚]` in `a[t̚]mosphere`.
    
- Hear `walking_on_eggshells` as one connected chunk.
    
- Remember that the final sound of `eggshells` is `/z/`.
    
- Keep `around_the boss` smooth and compressed.
    
- Learn `walk on eggshells` as a complete idiom rather than translating each word separately.
    

## Common Mistakes

- Do not pronounce every word with a separate pause.
    

```text
walking | on | eggshells
```

- Do not pronounce the final sound of `eggshells` as a sharp `/s/`.
    

```text
eggshells
→ final sound: /z/
```

- Do not release the `/t/` in `atmosphere` too strongly.
    

```text
a[t̚]mosphere
```

- Do not interpret `walking on eggshells` literally in this sentence.
