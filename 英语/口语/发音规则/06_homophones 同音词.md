## 1. 想解决什么问题？

为什么两个拼写和意思不同的词，发音却一样？

## 2. 核心概念

Homophones 是发音相同或非常接近，但拼写和意思不同的词。

## 3. PDF 例词整理

| 单词 1 | 单词 2 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| hole | whole | There was a hole in the road, and it took a whole afternoon to fix it. |
| where | wear | I don’t know where I’m going to wear the red scarf. |
| threw | through | The boy threw the ball through the window. |
| mail | male | A male postal worker usually delivers our mail. |
| piece | peace | Please give her a little piece of chocolate, and then we might have some peace. |
| whether | weather | Whether the weather will be good is uncertain. |

## 4. 容易错在哪里？

- 听力中容易把同音词听错。
- 写作中容易拼错。
- 口语中不需要强行区分发音，主要靠上下文区分意思。
